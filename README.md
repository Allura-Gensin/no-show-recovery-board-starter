# No-Show Recovery Board Starter

A small, client-owned spreadsheet framework for a non-clinical salon, hair-color, lash, brow, or esthetics business to review appointment exceptions without storing sensitive information.

This starter is deliberately a board and operating routine—not a reminder service, CRM, calendar integration, payment tool, or automated messaging product.

## Use it safely

1. Copy the three CSV files into a spreadsheet or board your business controls.
2. Use only an opaque customer reference or initials. Do not put medical, insurance, payment-card, government-ID, or other sensitive information in the board.
3. Replace the sample policy states with the policies your owner has approved.
4. Run the daily review before the opening hours that make sense for your schedule.
5. Your own staff, using your own approved sender and consent practices, decides whether any customer message is appropriate.

## Included files

- `appointment-exception-board.csv` — one row per appointment exception and its next owner action.
- `policy-ladder.csv` — a small policy-to-action map that the business must adapt and approve.
- `weekly-review.csv` — a lightweight owner review of the board process.

## Scope boundaries

The templates are for ordinary, non-clinical appointment operations only. They do not provide legal, privacy, compliance, medical, security, payment, or messaging advice. They do not send messages, connect to calendars, process payments, or make decisions about customers.

The business remains responsible for its own policies, records, access controls, customer communications, consent, and applicable law.
