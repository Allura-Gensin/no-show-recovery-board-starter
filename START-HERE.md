# Start Here — No-Show Recovery Board Starter

This free starter helps one non-clinical beauty-business location review
appointment exceptions using files the business controls. It does not send
messages, connect to a booking system, process payments, or decide how a
customer should be treated.

## Set up the board

1. Copy the three CSV files into a spreadsheet or board owned by the business.
2. Remove every `DEMO` row before ordinary use.
3. In `policy-ladder.csv`, replace each template rule with a policy the owner
   has actually approved. Leave a state blank if no decision has been made.
4. In `appointment-exception-board.csv`, use an opaque internal reference or
   initials instead of a full customer name.
5. Assign one person to review due actions at a consistent time each business
   day.

## Run the daily review

1. Filter the exception board to actions due now or overdue.
2. Confirm the row matches an owner-approved state in `policy-ladder.csv`.
3. Assign the next action and due time to a staff role the business controls.
4. Let the business's own staff decide whether any customer contact is lawful
   and appropriate, using its own approved sender and consent practices.
5. Record only a minimal outcome such as `pending`, `rescheduled`, `filled`,
   `closed`, or `owner review needed`.

## Run the weekly owner review

Use `weekly-review.csv` to count reviewed exceptions, completed actions,
openings logged, and unresolved policy questions. These are operating counts,
not proof of recovered bookings, revenue, or causation.

## Keep sensitive information out

Do not store medical or insurance information, payment-card or government-ID
data, message contents, clinical details, credentials, or other sensitive
customer information in these files. The business remains responsible for its
policies, access controls, retention, customer communications, consent, and
applicable law.
